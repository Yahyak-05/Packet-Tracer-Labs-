
## Day 2 Notes

This lab allowed me to apply the various things I've learned about different connectors, UTP copper connections, and the different types, for example, crossover cables and straight-through cables.

In this network topology, I've used the different types of cables to connect from end users to switches, from switches to routers, and even from switches to other switches, which required the crossover cable.

During this lab, I assumed Auto-MDIX was disabled in order to really grasp the fundamentals and not rely on automation.

Furthermore, when factoring in distances and cost, I also used fiber-optic cables: single-mode for longer distances and multimode for the shorter distances that are over 100 meters.

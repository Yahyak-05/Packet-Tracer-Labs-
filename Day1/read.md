# Lab Day 1: Basic Network Devices

Today, I learned about basic network devices and their purposes. This lab involved creating a simulation of a basic network topology that included an attacker.

## Devices Used

- 2 × Cisco 2911 Routers
- 2 × Cisco 2960 Switches
- 2 × Cisco ASA 5505 Firewalls
- 2 × PCs
- 2 × Servers
- 1 × Laptop used as the attacker in the diagram

## What I Learned

This lab helped me become familiar with the different devices commonly used in a network and the role each device plays. I also learned how these devices can be connected together to create a basic network topology.

The laptop was used to represent a potential attacker within the topology, helping demonstrate how an unauthorized device could potentially exist within or attempt to interact with a network.
